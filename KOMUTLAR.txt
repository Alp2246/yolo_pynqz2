--- GPS (NEO-6M, opsiyonel - konum etiketli tespit) ---
  Baglanti: NEO-6M -> USB-TTL (CH340/CP2102) -> kart USB
    VCC->5V, GND->GND, NEO TX->TTL RX
  Cihaz kontrol: ls /dev/ttyUSB0   (veya /dev/ttyACM0)
  Calistir (otomatik algilar):
    curl -s http://192.168.2.10:8000/update.sh | sh
  Elle:
    GPS_DEV=/dev/ttyUSB0 bash run_yolo_web.sh
    ./tiny_yolo_web /dev/ttyUSB0
  Panoda sag tarafta harita + koordinat + "son konumlu tespit" gorunur.
  Ilk fix icin acik gokyuzu / pencere kenari gerekir.
